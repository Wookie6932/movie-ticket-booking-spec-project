# My Evaluation

My first concern was keeping this assignment separate from the team project. I wanted my own copy so I could keep moving without changing anything the team was working on. Having the separate repository handled that.

During the build, the GitHub change view threw me off. It didn't look like a normal editor, and the temporary editing label made me question how the work was being saved. Opening the actual file and the readable page made the difference clear. That reinforced something useful for this project: the information can be correct, but if the presentation is unfamiliar, people can still feel unsure about using it.

I also checked the specification in desktop Chrome. After distinguishing the readable HTML page from the required Markdown link, I opened the `.md` version and confirmed it was live.

For the research, I wanted my wife's and daughter's responses kept in their own words, with the design decisions explained separately. Their feedback gave the project a clear direction: show the total earlier, make cancellation unmistakable, and give people a little more flexibility when reviewing their seats. Those improvements are now represented in the design; another review would help confirm how well they work.
